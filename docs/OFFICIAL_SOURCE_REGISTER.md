## Discovery limits

This worker retrieves every registered source on each run (the six NISR/LODA PDFs, the World Bank API, and landing pages of government, multilateral and evidence-library publishers). Landing pages prove reachability only; figures on them must be registered as specific documents before they support a finding. It does not search the whole web. Dataset-specific adapters are required before automatic analysis of new data. No access restriction is bypassed.

On update failures, the register keeps the failed check and separately records a previously verified cached copy only after validating its content hash. The original retrieval date and the uncertainty about newer versions remain visible.
