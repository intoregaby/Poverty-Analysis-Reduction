# NISR Judge Demonstration Script — Ethical and Verifiable

**Do not claim future features are already shipped.** The purpose is to demonstrate a complete connected system with visible source/data-state labels.

## Story: jobs -> skills -> real opportunity -> accountable support

1. National analyst opens poverty analysis; displays published EICV7 snapshot with source and date. Explain that poverty is survey-estimated, not derived from programme members.
2. Analyst identifies district programme reach gap; creates a **review request**, not an automatic beneficiary selection.
3. Programme manager accepts scoped request; sees active VUP programme with proper budget and agent recruitment tasks.
4. Recruiter publishes a sample paid field-agent job in a demo environment. An applicant completes a skills profile and application. Separate assessor reviews practical business/teaching skills; shows interview and human hiring decision.
5. Supervisor sees employee contract and trained agent; assigns a demo family who requested business support.
6. Agent mobile view shows assigned family and training goal. Agent conducts budgeting/pricing training, *helps the family implement* a soap-making, tailoring, farming or service microactivity, records member choice, and uploads consented evidence or a non-photo alternative.
7. Member confirms milestone in own profile; supervisor reviews on a different account. Show training attended vs actual project verified as **two distinct** results.
8. Finance view shows programme funding agreement, budget and separate support vs agent-payroll obligations; display `settled` **only if** live provider-confirmed test data is genuine. Otherwise show clear 'simulated' / 'not connected'.
9. Member chooses bank/MoMo education and account opening referral; display provider pending or independently confirmed only. Loan is an optional lender referral, not automatic eligibility.
10. Government authorized district view sees money reconciliation, number hired, members reached, skills demonstrated, projects implemented and disputed cases; demonstrate scoped access and privacy.
11. Return to original NISR analysis and export operational report with clear data source/period/status and separate evidence verification link.

## What judge should be able to ask

- Which values were actually observed in the survey versus entered by a programme?
- Who hired the agent, on what skills criteria, and when is their wage payable versus paid?
- What evidence proves a member **implemented** a business activity rather than merely attended training?
- Can a different agent or NGO access this family's record?
- Can you show actual provider reference proving funds arrived? If not, why does UI say pending?
- Does bank-account registration mean the family received credit or escaped poverty? (No.)
- Can the district officer see national household bank accounts? (No, not by default.)
- Does your proposed allocation outcome have validated causal effect? (No; scenario under assumptions.)

## Demo safety

Use fictional people and unlinked demo payment destinations; no actual bank transfers, no personal photos, no real beneficiaries' identities or unapproved survey microdata. If live provider integration is unavailable, use a visibly isolated simulation with source flags.
